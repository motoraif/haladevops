Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our DevOps services

How long does a typical CI/CD pipeline setup take?

Most CI/CD pipeline implementations take 2-4 weeks, depending on complexity. This includes initial assessment, design, implementation, testing, and team training. We work in sprints to deliver value incrementally.

Do you work with all cloud providers?

Yes! We have extensive experience with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. We'll recommend the best platform for your specific needs and budget, or work with your existing cloud infrastructure.

What's included in the DevOps Audit?

Our comprehensive audit includes: infrastructure review, security assessment, performance analysis, cost optimization opportunities, CI/CD pipeline evaluation, and a detailed report with actionable recommendations. You'll also get a 1-hour consultation to discuss findings.

Can you help with an existing infrastructure?

Absolutely! We specialize in optimizing and modernizing existing infrastructure. Whether you need to improve performance, reduce costs, enhance security, or implement better practices, we can help without disrupting your operations.

How much can I save with cost optimization?

Most clients see 30-60% reduction in cloud costs within the first 3 months. We identify unused resources, right-size instances, implement reserved instances, and optimize storage. The service typically pays for itself within the first month.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes! We offer flexible support packages including 24/7 monitoring, incident response, regular maintenance, and continuous optimization. We can also train your team to manage the infrastructure independently.

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with our work within the first week, we'll refund your payment in full. Our goal is to build long-term partnerships, not just complete projects.

How do I get started?

Simply book a free 30-minute consultation using the button above. We'll discuss your needs, challenges, and goals. Then we'll provide a detailed proposal with timeline and pricing. No commitment required for the initial consultation.
